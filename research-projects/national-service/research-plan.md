# Research plan — template and initial scope

Status: proposed; no data selected and no analysis conducted.

## Research question

How can paid national service expand youth opportunity, workforce development, and civic participation, and what does existing evidence suggest about program effectiveness and equitable access?

## Scope and subquestions

Investigate AmeriCorps and state-level service-year programs in the United States; record program type, purpose, eligibility, compensation, benefits, service length, geography, and period rather than pooling unlike programs automatically.

- Access: participation costs, foregone earnings, disability access, geography, education, caring responsibilities, and selection criteria.
- Opportunity: workforce development, educational outcomes, civic participation, and the measures used for each.
- Implementation: resources, retention, support, delivery, and differences across settings.
- Public investment: spending definitions, denominators, fiscal years, price adjustments, and comparability.

## Evidence and source plan

For each candidate source, verify publisher, title, date, URL, retrieval date, population, coverage, method, limitations, access conditions, and permitted use. Enter it in source-inventory.csv. Candidate sources are not evidence until checked. No studies or datasets have been selected yet.

Document inclusion and exclusion criteria before review: **to define**. Record search strategy, search dates, screening decisions, and contradictory evidence. Distinguish administrative documentation, descriptive statistics, observational studies, and causal evaluations.

## Data and measures

Complete data-dictionary.csv for each dataset: original variable, derived measure, definition, unit, population, time period, missing value codes, transformation, source identifier, and disclosure considerations. Record checksums and retrieval dates for permitted raw files. Preserve raw files unchanged locally.

## Analysis plan

Methods and comparisons: **to specify after source assessment**. Document confounding, selection, missingness, attrition, uncertainty, subgroup sample sizes, and sensitivity checks appropriate to the design. Do not imply causal effects from descriptive comparisons.

## Ethics and privacy

Use public nonidentifying data where appropriate. Confirm lawful use and publication permissions. If any participant information is proposed, establish consent, safeguarding, access, secure storage, retention, and ethical review before collection. Sensitive data must never enter this public repository, issue tracker, or rendered site.
